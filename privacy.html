Privacy — Matteo Backups

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

Matteo Backups is used only by its owner to back up their own files to their own Google Drive. It does not collect data from anyone else, and backup data is never shared, sold or used for advertising.

This website has no analytics, forms or sign-in; the hosting provider may keep ordinary web request logs.

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. For questions, use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen.
