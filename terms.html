Terms of use — Matteo Backups

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Matteo Backups is configured for personal use by its operator with files and a Google account he is authorized to use. It is not a public backup service and does not offer accounts to other users.

The operator controls which files are selected, when backups run, access to the Google account, and retention. Backup status must be checked and recovery should be tested; the presence of a scheduled job alone does not establish that a usable backup exists.

These pages describe the local tool and do not grant anyone access to its files or credentials. Use the support contact displayed on the Google consent screen for questions.
